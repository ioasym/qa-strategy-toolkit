# Risk Matrix

| Likelihood \ Impact | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| 4 | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 20 |
| 3 | 3 | 6 | 9 | 12 | 15 |
| 2 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 8 | 10 |
| 1 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

## Interpretation

- **16–25:** Critical
- **10–15:** High
- **5–9:** Medium
- **1–4:** Low

Use the matrix consistently, but document overrides. A security or regulatory obligation can warrant stronger treatment even when historical failure likelihood is low.
